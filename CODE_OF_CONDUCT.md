# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in pcdlint to be a good experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

- being respectful of differing opinions, experiences and viewpoints;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them publicly;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Behaviour that does not:

- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- insults, put-downs, trolling, or personal and political attacks;
- harassment of any kind, in public or in private;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by contacting
[@ZhiwarSajadi](https://github.com/ZhiwarSajadi) — privately, through a
GitHub message or a security advisory if you would rather it not be public.
All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take
fair, proportionate action in response to behaviour they consider
inappropriate, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers who do not follow or
enforce this code in good faith may face consequences determined by the
other maintainers.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repository, its issue
tracker, its pull requests, and any channel where someone is representing
the project — and continues after someone stops participating.
